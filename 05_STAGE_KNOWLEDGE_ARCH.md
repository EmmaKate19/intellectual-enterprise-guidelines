## 1. PURPOSE

Build the single source of truth for everything the organisation knows. Likely the most valuable asset in the enterprise. The principle: **everything known exists once, never duplicated.** Every course, article, book, AI protocol, and asset originates from one canonical knowledge source.

## 2. SCOPE — must define / produce

- [ ] The "single knowledge source" principle — what it means in practice
- [ ] Business Brain (NOT "second brain") — what it is, how it differs from a second brain
- [ ] Knowledge chain — how a piece of knowledge flows from origin → use → archive
- [ ] Taxonomy / classification — how knowledge is categorised across the enterprise
- [ ] De-duplication rule — the mechanism that prevents the same knowledge existing twice
- [ ] Relationship to AI agents (Book VIII) — how AIs read from / write to the knowledge source
- [ ] Relationship to ventures (Book VII) — how ventures consume and contribute knowledge
- [ ] Versioning of knowledge — how knowledge evolves without losing provenance
