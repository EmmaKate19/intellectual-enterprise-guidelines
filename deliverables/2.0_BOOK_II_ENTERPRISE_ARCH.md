## §A. Preamble — What This Book Does, and What It Deliberately Does Not

**Stability: Stable**

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §8.3 Book II; Constitution §K.1, §J.2.)*

This Book answers a single question: **what can exist inside the organisation?** The answer is *capabilities* — enduring organisational functions that outlive the businesses that temporarily express them (Constitution §K.2; Design Principle 3). This Book does not name companies. It does not design legal entities. It does not commit to a product roadmap. It does what the Constitution directed: it catalogues the capabilities, maps each to the outputs it can produce, and affirms the sequence in which the organisation is built before any company is.

The discipline of this Book is restraint. The temptation, when designing an enterprise, is to leap to the ventures — to name the clinic, the course, the supplement, the app — because ventures are concrete and capabilities are abstract. The architecture inverts this. Capabilities are designed first because they are what endures; ventures are designed later because they are what experiments (Constitution §H.2, §K.2). A venture that fails closes; the capability it expressed finds a new vehicle. This is the structural reason the organisation can experiment freely at the edge without losing its centre (Constitution §H.3).

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §K.2: "When a venture closes, the capability it expressed persists and finds a new vehicle.")*

This Book therefore defines the *possibility space* of the enterprise — the set of enduring functions the organisation is organised around — and explicitly defers the *populating* of that space (which ventures, which products, which companies) to downstream Books: the Venture Framework (Book VII), the Operating System (Book V), and Governance (Book III).

## §D. The Capability Catalogue

**Stability: Stable (the ratified set); Adaptive (definitions refine as the enterprise learns)**

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle + Founder Truth (source list).** *(Brief §5.3; Constitution §K.1; Document 0.0 §9 "Capability".)*

### D.1 What a capability is

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Document 0.0 §9; Brief §5.3; Constitution §K.2.)*

A **capability** is an enduring organisational function — a durable *ability to do something* — that outlives the businesses that temporarily express it. A capability is not a department, not a company, not a product line. It is the *competence* the organisation holds. When a venture closes, the capability it expressed persists and is re-expressed through a new vehicle (Constitution §K.2). Capabilities are the unit of organisational permanence; ventures are the unit of organisational experimentation.

The architecture hierarchy (Constitution §K.1) places capabilities directly beneath the Organisation and directly above Ventures. This is deliberate: it is the layer where permanence meets expression.

### D.2 The ratified capability set

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle (ratification of the Proposed list) + Founder Truth (the list originates in the founder's expressed intent).** *(Brief §5.3 Proposed list; Stage 03 §2 ratification requirement; Constitution §K.1.)*

The Brief (§5.3) proposed nine capabilities and marked them "Proposed — to be ratified in Book II." This Book ratifies the set, with one structural clarification (the relationship of Clinical to the others, and the status of Investment, addressed in §D.4 and §E). The ratified capability catalogue of the Intellectual Enterprise is:

| # | Capability | One-line definition | Stability |
|---|------------|---------------------|-----------|
| 1 | **Research** | The ability to investigate, validate, and synthesise knowledge at root-cause depth. | Stable |
| 2 | **Education** | The ability to transmit transformational knowledge and build capacity in others. | Stable |
| 3 | **Technology** | The ability to build and operate software, AI agents, automations, and digital systems. | Stable |
| 4 | **Clinical** | The ability to apply health knowledge to individual cases — consultations, protocols, practitioner craft. | Stable |
| 5 | **Publishing** | The ability to produce durable written works — books, frameworks, workbooks, journals. | Stable |
| 6 | **Media** | The ability to produce and distribute spoken/visual content — podcast, video, newsletter, speaking. | Stable |
| 7 | **Commerce** | The ability to design, source, and sell physical/digital goods — supplements, tools, merchandise. | Stable |
| 8 | **Investment** | The ability to allocate capital — into the enterprise's own ventures and, selectively, into aligned external ones. | Stable (intent); Adaptive (mechanics deferred) |
| 9 | **Impact** | The ability to generate public good beyond the market — charity, scholarships, advocacy, access. | Stable |

> **Decision audit (DEC-BII-001 — ratify the nine-capability set):** *Why?* The Constitution (§K.1) directs Book II to catalogue capabilities; the Brief (§5.3) proposed this set; the founder's expressed venture intentions (health, education, writing, technology, products, giving) map onto it without residue. *Alternatives considered:* (a) a coarser 5-capability set (collapsing Publishing+Media, Clinical+Education) — rejected because it obscures the distinct Flywheel expressions and distinct output types of each; (b) a finer 12+ set (splitting Technology into Software/AI/Data) — rejected as premature; Book VIII will design the AI sub-architecture, and over-specifying now reduces future optionality (violates Design Principle 9). *Why this?* Nine capabilities are the minimum sufficient set that (i) covers every venture the founder has expressed interest in, (ii) gives each a distinct Flywheel expression (§F), and (iii) does not pre-empt downstream Books. *Assumptions:* the founder's venture interests are stable in *kind* (transformation, health, education, creation) even as they evolve in *form*. *Risks:* a capability may later prove to be two capabilities, or two may prove to be one — mitigated by Adaptive definitions and the §5.4 change mechanism. *When it would no longer serve:* if the enterprise's work converges onto fewer than four enduring functions, or diverges onto more than twelve, the catalogue is re-ratified.

### D.3 Why these nine — and not the superseded five

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.3 vs §5.6; Stage 03 §4.)*

The earlier "five divisions" model (Knowledge → Education → Innovation → Operations → Impact, Brief §5.6) was superseded by the capability model (Brief §5.3) for a structural reason: the five-division model mixed *levels of abstraction*. "Knowledge" and "Education" are capabilities; "Innovation" and "Operations" are *processes* that cut across all capabilities; "Impact" is a capability but was inconsistently scoped. The capability model separates the *what* (capabilities) from the *how* (processes — which are the Flywheel, §F, and the Operating System, Book V). This is why the supersession is recorded as architectural, not merely a rename (§H).

### D.4 Clinical — a capability expressed through projects, not a division

> **Evidence label: Founder Truth + Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.6: "The clinic is explicitly a project, not a division"; Stage 03 §4.)*

The Clinical capability is ratified as an enduring competence — the ability to apply health knowledge to individual cases. The founder's clinic, however, is a *project* (a specific initiative within a product within a venture), not a division and not the whole of the Clinical capability. A clinic is one vehicle through which Clinical is expressed; the capability also encompasses protocols, practitioner training, and consultation craft that may be expressed through other vehicles (digital consults, training programs, written protocols). Affirming Clinical as a capability while affirming the clinic as a project preserves both the permanence of the competence and the experimental, retireable nature of the specific vehicle.

### D.5 Investment — the capability whose mechanics are most deferred

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.3 listed Investment in the capability set but provided no output mapping — see §E.8.)*

Investment is ratified as a capability: the ability to allocate capital. Its inclusion reflects the enterprise's both/and commercial frame (Constitution §N) — the enterprise is profit-maximising *and* mission-sustaining, and allocating capital (into its own ventures and selectively into aligned external ones) is how profit is recycled into mission. The *mechanics* of Investment — what vehicles, what governance, what criteria — are heavily deferred (to Books III, VII, and IX). This Book ratifies the *capability exists*; it does not design the portfolio.

## §E. The Capability → Output Mapping

**Stability: Stable (the kinds of outputs); Adaptive (specific outputs evolve)**

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle (ratification/refinement of the Proposed mapping) + Founder Truth (output types originate in the founder's expressed intent).** *(Brief §5.3 mapping; Stage 03 §2.)*

The Brief (§5.3) provided a partial output mapping and marked it Proposed. This Book ratifies the mapping with refinements: it completes the **Investment** mapping (which the Brief left blank), sharpens each capability's outputs against the Flywheel (§F) and the single-source-of-truth principle (Constitution §K.3; Design Principle 2), and labels each output's evidence standard. The mapping describes *what each capability can produce* — not what it currently produces. Specific products and ventures are Book VII work.

### E.1 Research → outputs

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle + Founder Truth.** *(Brief §5.3; Founder Constitution — root-cause truth as a centre value.)*

Research produces: **articles, evidence summaries, original frameworks, validated protocols, and the knowledge substrate that every other capability consumes.** Research is the upstream capability: it is where the Flywheel's *Observe → Understand* is most concentrated (§F). Its outputs are not consumer products; they are the raw material of Education, Clinical, Publishing, and Media. This reflects Design Principle 2 (knowledge created once, expressed many times) — Research creates the "once"; downstream capabilities do the "many times."

### E.2 Education → outputs

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle + Founder Truth.** *(Brief §5.3; Founder Constitution §06, §08 — transformational education, not commodity courses; Constitution §I.2 exclusion 5.)*

Education produces: **courses, workshops, certification, membership programs, and capacity-building experiences.** Every output is transformation-focused, not commodity-framed (Constitution §I.2, exclusion 5: the prohibition is on commodity framing and pressure-marketing, not on courses themselves). Education is where the Flywheel's *Teach* is most concentrated. The founder foresees delivering many courses; each is a vehicle for transformational education, and the capability endures across all of them.

### E.3 Technology → outputs

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.3; Constitution §O — AI accountability.)*

Technology produces: **AI assistants, applications, automations, software systems, and digital infrastructure.** Technology is the scaling lever — it is how transformation scales without losing integrity (Constitution §K.3: "systems that allow that transformation to scale"). All AI outputs are bound by the AI Accountability Principle (Constitution §O): AI augments judgement; it does not replace accountability. The detailed AI architecture is Book VIII; this Book ratifies Technology as the capability that *houses* it.

### E.4 Clinical → outputs

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle + Founder Truth.** *(Brief §5.3; Founder Constitution — naturopathic practice; §D.4 above.)*

Clinical produces: **consultations, health protocols, practitioner training, and case-based knowledge.** The clinic (a project) is one expression; the capability spans all individualised application of health knowledge. Clinical is where the Flywheel's *Observe → Understand → Prepare* is applied to a specific person. Clinical outputs feed back into Research (case evidence) — a closed loop (§F).

### E.5 Publishing → outputs

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle + Founder Truth.** *(Brief §5.3; Founder Constitution — the founder as author/writer; written-first energy-protective mode, Constitution §M.3.)*

Publishing produces: **books, workbooks, frameworks (as published artefacts), journals, and durable written works.** Publishing is distinct from Media (§E.6): Publishing is the *durable, written* expression; Media is the *ephemeral, spoken/visual* expression. Both derive from the same knowledge-once source (Design Principle 2). Publishing is the most energy-protective public-presence mode (Constitution §M.3: written-first always) and is therefore the primary mode by which the founder surfaces publicly.

### E.6 Media → outputs

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle + Founder Truth.** *(Brief §5.3; Founder Constitution §04; Constitution §M.3.)*

Media produces: **podcast, video, newsletter, speaking engagements, and spoken/visual content.** Media is the *live and ephemeral* expression — and therefore the one most constrained by the energy-protection gate (Constitution §M.3: rehearse-live, start-with-trusted-audiences). Media is secondary to Publishing in the founder's public-presence architecture (§M.3: written-first always, live secondary), not because it is less valuable but because it is more energy-expensive. Any Media venture that requires sustained, unrehearsed, high-frequency public performance is off-mission (Constitution §I.2, exclusion 2).

### E.7 Commerce → outputs

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle + Founder Truth.** *(Brief §5.3; Founder Constitution — 34-year plant-based living; ethical boundaries Constitution §G.)*

Commerce produces: **supplements, tools, merchandise, and physical/digital goods.** Commerce is the most physically-supply-chain-intensive capability and is therefore the most exposed to the ethical boundary gate (Constitution §G: no intentional harm to people or animals). Every Commerce output must be testable against the supply chain for harm (Constitution §I.2, exclusion 3). Commerce expresses the both/and frame (Constitution §N): it is a profit vehicle that sustains the mission engine.

### E.8 Investment → outputs (mapping completed here — the Brief left it blank)

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.3 listed Investment but provided no mapping; this Book completes it.)*

Investment produces: **capital allocation into the enterprise's own ventures (internal investment), selective capital allocation into aligned external ventures (external investment), and the financial infrastructure that recycles profit into mission.** Investment is the capability that operationalises the both/and frame at the capital level (Constitution §N): profit is maximised *as a vehicle*, and a portion is recycled *into the engine* (mission-sustaining). The criteria for "aligned external" ventures are the Captain's Test and the on/off-mission gate (Constitution §I) — Investment does not invest in off-mission ventures, even profitable ones. The governance of Investment (who decides, what thresholds, what vehicles) is deferred to Books III, VII, and IX.

> **Decision audit (DEC-BII-002 — complete the Investment mapping):** *Why?* The Brief listed Investment as a capability but left its output mapping blank, leaving the catalogue internally incomplete. *Alternatives:* (a) defer Investment entirely to a later Book — rejected because a capability catalogue with an undefined member is a draft error (Document 0.0 §4.2: no orphan claims); (b) define Investment narrowly as "the enterprise's own ventures only" — rejected because the both/and frame (Constitution §N) and the founder's long-term intent (impact at scale) imply selective external allocation is within the possibility space. *Why this?* Defining Investment as internal + selective-external, with mechanics deferred, ratifies the *capability* without pre-empting the *portfolio* — preserving optionality (Design Principle 9). *Assumptions:* the enterprise will eventually hold capital beyond immediate operating needs. *Risks:* Investment could drift toward profit-without-mission; mitigated by the explicit "aligned external" criterion (Captain's Test) and the deferral of mechanics to Governance (Book III). *When it would no longer serve:* if the enterprise never holds investable capital, Investment is held dormant as a capability — not removed (capabilities persist; §D.1).

### E.9 Impact → outputs

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle + Founder Truth.** *(Brief §5.3; Founder Constitution — empower the vulnerable and underdogs; Constitution §P.2, §E.2.6.)*

Impact produces: **charity, scholarships, advocacy, access programs, and public-good initiatives that operate beyond the market.** Impact is the capability that most directly expresses the founder's purpose ("empower and build capacity, especially the vulnerable and underdogs"). Impact is not a downstream residue of profit; it is a ratified capability with its own outputs. The specific structures (charity entity, scholarship mechanics) are deferred to Book IX (Legacy) and Book III (Governance); this Book ratifies the *capability exists* and the *kinds of outputs* it produces.

### E.10 The mapping as a possibility space, not a commitment

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Stage 03 §2: "No businesses yet — only capabilities." Constitution §J.3.)*

The mapping describes what each capability *can* produce. It is not a product roadmap, not a venture list, and not a commitment to produce every output. A capability may be held dormant for years before a venture expresses it; that does not diminish its status as a capability (§D.1: capabilities persist). The mapping is the possibility space; the populating of it is downstream Book work.

## §F. How Capabilities Interlock — The Flywheel Across the Catalogue

**Stability: Stable**

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §L — the Enterprise Flywheel; Design Principle 2 — knowledge created once, expressed many times.)*

### F.1 Every capability expresses the Flywheel

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §L.3: "Every venture, every capability, every system expresses it.")*

The Constitution (§L.3) states that the Enterprise Flywheel (Observe → Understand → Prepare → Create → Teach → Refine → Observe) is the operating rhythm of the enterprise and that *every capability expresses it*. This Book makes that concrete: each capability is a lens through which the same six-stage loop turns. The loop is not six separate capabilities; it is one rhythm expressed nine ways.

### F.2 Where each Flywheel stage concentrates

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §L.2; this Book's §E.)*

| Flywheel stage | Capabilities where it concentrates | Why |
|----------------|-------------------------------------|-----|
| **Observe** | Research · Clinical · Impact | Listening, sensing need — before acting (Constitution §L.2). |
| **Understand** | Research · Clinical | Root-cause depth (founder's centre value). |
| **Prepare** | Clinical · Education · Technology | Designing the response before delivering it. |
| **Create** | Publishing · Media · Technology · Commerce | Producing the artefact, system, or good. |
| **Teach** | Education · Media · Publishing | Transmitting to those who need it. |
| **Refine** | Research · Technology · Investment | Reviewing, improving, recycling learning (and capital) back in. |

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(This table is an operationalisation of Constitution §L, not a new principle.)*

No capability is confined to one stage; the table marks *concentration*, not exclusion. Education, for instance, Observe-Understand-Prepare-Create-Teach-Refine in full — it simply *concentrates* at Teach. The point of the table is to show the catalogue is internally coherent: the nine capabilities are not nine silos but nine expressions of one loop.

### F.3 The knowledge-once loop — capabilities as a single circulation

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.7; Constitution §K.3; Design Principle 2.)*

The single-source-of-truth principle (Constitution §K.3; Design Principle 2) means the capabilities form a circulation, not a pipeline:

```
Research creates knowledge (the "once")
        ↓
Education · Clinical · Publishing · Media · Technology express it (the "many times")
        ↓
Commerce · Investment sustain it (the vehicles)
        ↓
Impact radiates it (beyond the market)
        ↓
Research refines it (case evidence, feedback) → back to the top
```

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.7 canonical chain; Constitution §L.3.)*

This is the architectural reason no capability is redundant: each is a distinct expression of the same knowledge, and each feeds back into Research. A venture that does not plug into this circulation — that creates knowledge independently or duplicates it — violates Design Principle 2 and the Business Brain principle (Document 0.0 §9; Book IV).

## §G. Organisation Before Companies — The Sequence, Made Concrete

**Stability: Stable**

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.4; Constitution §K.1; Stage 03 §2.)*

### G.1 The ratified sequence

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.4.)*

The Brief (§5.4) established the sequence rule: legal structures must not be designed first. This Book affirms and makes it concrete by placing the capability catalogue (this Book) as the second step in the sequence:

```
Organisation  →  Capabilities  →  Governance  →  Ownership philosophy  →  Legal entities  →  Companies
   (Book I)       (Book II)         (Book III)      (Book III / IX)         (post-III)          (Book VII)
```

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.4; Stage 03 §2 affirms the sequence.)*

This Book occupies the second position. It is complete and ratified before Governance (Book III) begins. The sequence is not a suggestion; it is the structural reason the enterprise avoids premature restructuring (Brief §5.4: "This sequencing reduces restructuring later").

### G.2 Why the sequence is load-bearing

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.4; Design Principle 9 — increase future optionality.)*

If legal entities are designed before capabilities, the legal shape constrains the capability shape — and capabilities outlive legal entities (Constitution §K.2). Designing capabilities first means the legal structure is later *fitted to* the capabilities, not the reverse. This protects optionality (Design Principle 9): the enterprise can choose the legal vehicle that best expresses a capability *when it is ready to express it*, rather than being locked into a vehicle chosen before the capability was understood.

> **Decision audit (DEC-BII-003 — affirm the organisation-before-companies sequence with Book positions):** *Why?* The sequence was Established in the Brief but not yet mapped to the Book structure; mapping it makes the inter-Book dependency explicit and prevents a downstream stage from skipping a step. *Alternatives:* leave the sequence unmapped — rejected because unmapped dependencies become orphaned dependencies (the single biggest long-term risk per HANDOVER_PROTOCOL §5). *Why this?* Mapping each step to its Book makes the sequence machine-checkable: a future AI agent can verify that no Book attempts a downstream step before its upstream step is ratified. *Assumptions:* the Book sequence (I→II→III→…→IX) is the production sequence (Constitution §J.3; Document 0.0 §12.3). *Risks:* a step may need to be revisited (e.g., Governance informs a capability refinement) — the §5.4 change mechanism handles this; the sequence is the default, not a one-way valve. *When it would no longer serve:* never — the principle (capabilities before legal entities) is time-invariant; only the Book mapping could update.

### G.3 What this Book does NOT do in the sequence

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.5; Constitution §J.3.)*

This Book ratifies the *capability* layer and affirms the *sequence*. It does not design Ownership philosophy, Legal entities, or Companies — those are explicitly downstream (§C.2). The founder's earlier discussion of entity structures (Sole Trader, Pty Ltd, Family Trust, Holding, Trust+Company) is recorded as *deferred to professional advice after Books II & III* (Brief §5.5), and this Book does not pre-empt it.

## §H. Supersession Record — The 5-Division Model

**Stability: Immutable (the supersession is a historical fact, never to be reversed)**

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.6.)*

It is not renamed; it is replaced, for the structural reason given in §D.3: the five-division model mixed levels of abstraction (capabilities with processes), whereas the capability model separates them.

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Brief §5.6.)*

Nothing else from the five-division model is carried forward.

### H.3 The rule against resurrection

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Stage 03 §4: "do not resurrect it"; Document 0.0 §10.2.)*

The five-division model is not to be resurrected. If a future collaborator or AI agent proposes restoring it, the proposal is a §5.4 change request (Document 0.0 §5.4) routed through the Founder — not a silent reversion. The supersession is recorded here as institutional memory: the enterprise remembers *why* it moved from five divisions to nine capabilities, so the mistake is not remade (Document 0.0 §10.2).

## §I. The Capability Layer Within the Architecture Hierarchy

**Stability: Stable**

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §K.1; Brief §5.2.)*

### I.1 Where capabilities sit

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §K.1.)*

The Constitution (§K.1) fixes the hierarchy: Founder → Organisation → **Capabilities** → Ventures → Products → Projects. This Book does not move capabilities within that hierarchy; it *populates* the capability layer. The hierarchy is now concrete:

```
Founder (Emma)
   ↓
Organisation (the Intellectual Enterprise)
   ↓
Capabilities (Research · Education · Technology · Clinical · Publishing · Media · Commerce · Investment · Impact)  ← this Book
   ↓
Ventures (businesses expressing one or more capabilities)  ← Book VII
   ↓
Products (what ventures produce)  ← Book VII
   ↓
Projects (specific initiatives within products)  ← Book VII
```

### I.2 The many-to-many relationship: capabilities ↔ ventures

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §K.2: "A venture is a business that expresses one or more capabilities.")*

A venture expresses *one or more* capabilities (Constitution §K.2). A capability is expressed by *zero or more* ventures (a capability may be held dormant, §E.10). The relationship is many-to-many:

- A single venture (e.g., a future health-education platform) may express Research + Education + Technology + Clinical simultaneously.
- A single capability (e.g., Publishing) may be expressed by multiple ventures (a book imprint, a journal, a workbook series).

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(This operationalises Constitution §K.2; it introduces no new principle.)*

This is why capabilities are the permanent layer and ventures the experimental layer: the many-to-many mapping can be reconfigured freely (ventures pivot, close, launch) while the capabilities persist. Designing the catalogue before the ventures is what makes the reconfiguration safe (§G.2).

### I.3 What this Book does not populate

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §J.3; Stage 03 §2.)*

This Book populates the *capability* layer only. It does not populate the Ventures, Products, or Projects layers — those are Book VII. Any specific venture named in this Book (none are named) would be a scope violation. The architecture hierarchy is filled top-down, one layer per Book, in sequence (§G.1).

## §J. How the Constitutional Gates Apply to Capabilities

**Stability: Stable**

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §I, §L, §M, §G; this Book applies them, it does not amend them.)*

The Constitution establishes several gates that bind "every venture, every role, every system, and every AI agent" (§M.2). This section makes explicit how those gates apply at the *capability* layer — the layer above ventures. Capabilities are upstream of ventures; if a capability is sound, the ventures that express it inherit a sound foundation. If a capability itself violated a constitutional gate, every venture expressing it would inherit the violation. The gates therefore apply at the capability layer as *design constraints on what may be catalogued*.

### J.1 The on/off-mission gate (Constitution §I) — applied to capabilities

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §I.1, §I.2, §I.3.)*

Each ratified capability is testable against the Captain's Test (Constitution §I.1) and the off-mission exclusions (§I.2). The test at the capability layer is: *does this capability, by its nature, serve the founder's purpose — helping people climb out of the dark, break conditioning, and expand into new dimensions of living — and is it free of the five off-mission exclusions?*

| Capability | Captain's Test | Off-mission exclusion check | Verdict |
|------------|----------------|------------------------------|---------|
| Research | Serves root-cause truth (founder's centre value) | None triggered | On-mission |
| Education | Serves transformational capacity-building | Exclusion 5 (commodity courses) — *does not trigger at capability level*; triggers at venture level if a specific course is commodity-framed | On-mission (capability); ventures vetted individually |
| Technology | Serves scaling transformation without losing integrity | None at capability level; AI accountability (§O) binds at venture level | On-mission |
| Clinical | Serves individual health transformation | None | On-mission |
| Publishing | Serves durable transmission of knowledge (written-first, energy-protective) | None | On-mission |
| Media | Serves spoken/visual transmission | Exclusion 2 (performative persona) — *does not trigger at capability level*; triggers at venture level if a Media venture requires sustained unrehearsed performance | On-mission (capability); ventures vetted individually |
| Commerce | Serves goods that support transformation | Exclusion 3 (harm to people/animals) — *triggers at venture/product level via supply chain* | On-mission (capability); ventures vetted individually |
| Investment | Serves recycling profit into mission | None at capability level; "aligned external" criterion (§E.8) binds at venture level | On-mission |
| Impact | Directly serves empowering the vulnerable | None | On-mission |

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(This table applies Constitution §I to the catalogue; it introduces no new gate.)*

A crucial distinction emerges: **some off-mission exclusions do not trigger at the capability level but do trigger at the venture level.** Education (the capability) is on-mission; a *commodity-framed course* (a venture) is off-mission (Constitution §I.2, exclusion 5). Media (the capability) is on-mission; a *sustained-unrehearsed-performance* media venture is off-mission (exclusion 2). Commerce (the capability) is on-mission; a *harmful-supply-chain* product is off-mission (exclusion 3). This is why the on/off-mission gate is applied *twice* — once at the capability layer (this Book) and once at the venture layer (Book VII). The capability-level check ensures the *possibility space* is sound; the venture-level check ensures each *instance* is sound.

### J.2 The energy-protection gate (Constitution §M) — applied to capabilities

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle + Founder Truth.** *(Constitution §M.2, §M.3.)*

The energy-protection gate (Constitution §M) binds every venture, role, system, and AI agent. At the capability layer, it applies as a *design constraint on how each capability may be expressed*:

- **Publishing** is the most energy-protective capability (written-first, Constitution §M.3) and is therefore the *primary* mode of the founder's public presence. The architecture does not require the founder to be visible live in order for Publishing to function.
- **Media** is the most energy-intensive capability (live, ephemeral) and is therefore *secondary*, constrained by rehearse-live and start-with-trusted-audiences (§M.3). The architecture permits Media to be held dormant or lightly expressed without diminishing the enterprise.
- **Clinical** is energy-intensive in its live, one-to-one form; the architecture permits Clinical to be expressed through lower-energy vehicles (written protocols, recorded training, asynchronous consults) where the live clinic would violate energy protection.
- **Research, Education, Technology, Commerce, Investment, Impact** are expressed primarily through systems and written/designed work — the founder's energy is spent in *creation and design*, not in sustained live delivery. This is the architectural expression of Design Principle 7 (protect founder energy) and Constitution §M.2.

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §M.3: written-first always; §M.2: scale is a system outcome, not founder velocity.)*

The architectural implication: **the capability catalogue is designed so that the enterprise can function at scale with the founder expending energy primarily in written creation and design, not in sustained live performance.** A capability whose only viable expression required sustained, unrehearsed, high-frequency founder performance would be off-mission (Constitution §I.2, exclusion 2) — and no capability in this catalogue has that requirement, because each has a written-first or system-mediated expression path.

### J.3 The Flywheel gate (Constitution §L) — applied to capabilities

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §L.3.)*

Every capability must express the Flywheel (Constitution §L.3). §F demonstrates that each does. A capability that could not be expressed through Observe → Understand → Prepare → Create → Teach → Refine → Observe would not belong in the catalogue. None of the nine fails this test (§F.2).

### J.4 The ethical-boundary gate (Constitution §G) — applied to capabilities

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §G.1, §G.4.)*

No capability may, by its nature, require intentional harm or dishonesty (Constitution §G.1). None of the nine does. The ethical-boundary gate binds most concretely at the venture and product layer (supply chains, marketing honesty — Book VII), but the capability layer is pre-cleared: the catalogue contains no capability whose *essence* is harmful or deceptive.

### J.5 The AI-accountability principle (Constitution §O) — applied to capabilities

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Constitution §O.1, §O.2.)*

The Technology capability houses the enterprise's AI outputs (§E.3). At the capability layer, the AI Accountability Principle (Constitution §O) is a *design constraint*: Technology is ratified as a capability *on the condition* that every AI output it produces keeps a human accountable party (Constitution §O.1). The operational AI architecture is Book VIII; this Book ratifies the capability and inherits the principle.
