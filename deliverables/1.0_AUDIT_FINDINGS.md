## §A. Audit Purpose & Method

> **Evidence label: Architectural Principle.** *(Document 0.0 §15; Brief §15.)*

The Audit Phase examines the Founder Constitution and identifies five things, *before* any Book I drafting: (1) immutable principles, (2) emerging organisational principles, (3) areas requiring clarification, (4) potential contradictions, (5) missing governance elements. The audit produces a findings document for Founder review; only after it is agreed does Book I drafting begin.

Method applied: a full read of the audit subject was conducted; each element was classified against (a) Document 0.0 §8 Stability Classification (Immutable / Stable / Adaptive / Experimental), (b) the Stable Core vs Adaptive Edge distinction (Brief §5.8), and (c) the eleven Design Principles and six quality gates (Document 0.0 §3, §6). Cross-reference was made to the Brief §5.9 Flywheel proposal, Brief §8.3 Book I scope, and Brief §17 status register.

Scope note. *(Architectural Principle — Document 0.0 §9.)* The Founder Constitution is defined as the *Immutable source of Emma's identity, values, mission, voice, psychology*. The audit therefore asks two distinct questions throughout: **(i)** what in this document is immutable identity that Book I must *carry verbatim and protect*; and **(ii)** what in this document is personal-brand or operational expression that Book I must *elevate* (translate from "I/Emma" to "the Organisation") without contradicting the source. Book I is an Organisational Constitution; the Founder Constitution remains the immutable parent of the founder's identity. These are two documents with a defined relationship, not one document renamed.
