## 1. PURPOSE

Design the AI workforce — the digital executive team. Define each AI role's authority, knowledge access, responsibilities, and escalation rules so the organisation can operate with AI agents as a first-class workforce, not as ad-hoc assistants.

## 2. SCOPE — must define / produce

- [ ] The AI roster (which roles exist — e.g., chief of staff, researcher, brand custodian, knowledge keeper, operations manager)
- [ ] Per-AI attributes (for each role):
  - [ ] Role & title
  - [ ] Authority (what it can decide / do autonomously)
  - [ ] Knowledge access (what it may read from the single knowledge source, Stage 05)
  - [ ] Responsibilities (what it owns end-to-end)
  - [ ] Escalation rules (when it must escalate to the founder or another AI)
- [ ] AI-to-AI interaction protocol (how agents hand off, collaborate, avoid conflict)
- [ ] Human-in-the-loop gates (where founder sign-off is required before AI action)
- [ ] AI lifecycle (how an AI role is created, updated, retired)
- [ ] Alignment to founder identity (every AI must operate within the Digital Twin Core, 00_DIGITAL_TWIN_CORE.md)

## 3. INPUTS

- 00_GLOBAL_INDEX.md, 00_DIGITAL_TWIN_CORE.md (background — AIs must align to founder identity)
- Stage 08 handover manifest (Venture Framework — defines where AIs plug into the lifecycle)
- Project_Genesis_and_Continuation_Brief.md §6.3 (future AI workforce — proposed, to be designed here) and §8.3 (Book VIII intent)

## 4. CONSTRAINTS

- AI roles are **proposed future work** (per §6.3) — this is the stage where they are formally designed. Do not build/deploy AIs in this stage; define the architecture.
- Must honour the founder's energy constraint — the AI workforce exists to reduce founder load, not add management overhead.
- Every AI's authority must respect the Governance (Stage 04) — AIs do not override governance; they execute within it.
