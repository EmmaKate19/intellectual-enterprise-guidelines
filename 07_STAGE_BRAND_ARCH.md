## 1. PURPOSE

Define where the founder's personal-brand work plugs into the enterprise, and establish the full brand hierarchy. Each level of the hierarchy has a different purpose — this stage clarifies the relationships and rules so brand execution never conflicts with the organisation's identity.

## 2. SCOPE — must define / produce

- [ ] The brand hierarchy (canonical): Emma (Founder) → Organisation → Personal Brand → Business Brands → Product Brands → Programs → Campaigns
- [ ] Purpose of each level (why each exists, what it does, what it must NOT do)
- [ ] How the founder's current personal-brand work (the Digital Twin / Master Document) plugs in
- [ ] Visual identity governance — how "Grounded Ethereal" scales from personal to organisational to product level
- [ ] Voice governance — dual voice (private/strategic vs public/external) across hierarchy levels
- [ ] Brand-to-venture relationship (how a venture gets its brand — when, who decides)
- [ ] Naming authority (who names what — ties to Operating System, Stage 06)
- [ ] Brand audit / drift detection (how to know when a venture's brand has drifted from the hierarchy)

## 4. CONSTRAINTS

- The hierarchy is **established intent** — ratify it, do not redesign it. If the founder wants to change the hierarchy, use the change process (see Global Index §4 Rule 5 / Charter governance).
- Must honour the founder's energy constraint — brand execution should be delegable to AIs/contractors once the architecture is set.
- Brand Architecture depends on Knowledge Architecture (Stage 05) and Operating System (Stage 06) — do not define mechanics that belong there.
